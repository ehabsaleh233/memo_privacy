ESK operates Games Castle (قلعة الألعاب). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the application, including its games, multiplayer rooms, chat, voice communication, advertisements, and purchase features.

1. Information We Collect

The information processed depends on the features you use.

Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we receive account information such as your unique user identifier, display name, email address, and profile picture.

Authentication is handled through Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. We do not receive your Google account password.

Some features may be available without signing in. Guest access may still involve technical information, locally stored preferences, or an anonymous identifier where applicable.

Game and Activity Information

We may process information necessary to operate gameplay and account features, including:

  • Game progress, scores, results, and completed levels.
  • Multiplayer room identifiers and membership.
  • Game state and player interactions.
  • Virtual currency balances, rewards, and inventory.
  • Purchase and reward transaction records.
  • Language, sound, and other preferences.

Some information is stored locally on your device, while account-related information may be stored on our service providers’ systems.

Chat Messages

When you send messages in chat rooms, we may store the message content, your account identifier, display name, and associated timestamps.

Messages are visible to other users who can access the relevant chat room.

Voice Communication

If you enable your microphone, your audio is processed and transmitted through Agora to provide real-time voice communication.

Other participants connected to the same voice channel can hear you while your microphone is enabled.

The current application does not implement an audio recording feature. However, other participants may independently record conversations using their own devices or software.

Device and Advertising Information

The application and its integrated service providers may process technical information such as:

  • IP address.
  • Device model and operating system.
  • Application version.
  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.
  • Connection, diagnostic, and service error information.
  • Advertisement impressions and interactions.

The information processed depends on the services enabled, device settings, applicable requirements, and consent choices.

Purchase Information

When you make purchases through Google Play, we may process product identifiers, purchase tokens, transaction status, and refund information to verify purchases and deliver the corresponding items or benefits.

Google Play handles payment processing. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Reports and Blocking

If you report another user, we store your account identifier, the reported user’s identifier, the reason you selected, any details you add, the related room code, and, when you report a message, the reported message text. Reports are used only to review and act on abuse.

If you block another user, the blocked user’s identifier and display name are stored in your account so their messages can be hidden from you. You can unblock users at any time from your profile.

Support Information

If you contact us, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address, account identifier, and details of your request.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Authenticate users and operate accounts.
  • Provide games and multiplayer features.
  • Enable chat and voice communication.
  • Save progress, balances, inventory, and preferences.
  • Process and verify purchases and rewards.
  • Detect duplicate transactions, fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the application.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Information Visible to Other Users

Depending on the features you use, other users may see your display name, profile picture, room participation, messages, game activity, and results.

When you enable voice communication, participants in the same voice channel may hear you.

Avoid sharing passwords, payment information, home addresses, or other sensitive personal information through chat or voice features.

Other users may copy, save, or share information you disclose to them.

Advertising providers may process device information, advertising identifiers, and advertising interactions to deliver advertisements, measure performance, and prevent fraud, subject to applicable requirements and privacy choices.

Where required, consent must be obtained before processing information for personalized advertising. Advertising involving children is subject to additional restrictions.

6. Permissions and Local Storage

Microphone Permission

Microphone access is used for voice communication. You can deny or revoke this permission through your device settings. Doing so prevents microphone use but does not necessarily prevent access to games without voice communication.

Internet Access

An internet connection is used for online gameplay, authentication, chat, voice, advertisements, and purchases.

Local Storage

The application stores preferences and certain game information locally on your device. Clearing application data or uninstalling the application may remove this locally stored information.

Removing the application does not automatically delete server-side account information.

Games Castle includes games intended for a broad audience, which may include children.

Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of online features, especially chat, voice communication, advertisements, and purchases.

Children should not share personal information, including their full name, address, school, contact details, or passwords, with other users.

Where applicable law requires parental consent for processing a child’s information, that processing requires the appropriate consent or another legally permitted basis.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about information associated with your child, contact developera44@gmail.com. We will review the request, verify the relevant relationship where necessary, and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored on your device and on systems operated by our service providers. These systems may be located outside your country of residence.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account information and saved progress: While needed to maintain the account and provide its features, subject to deletion requests.
  • Room information and chat messages: While needed to operate the relevant features, address support requests, or investigate abuse.
  • Purchase and reward records: While needed to verify transactions, prevent duplicate credits, process refunds, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations.
  • Support and diagnostic information: While needed to resolve issues, maintain service reliability, or address security concerns.

Retention is based on these purposes rather than one fixed period for every category.

Some information may remain in provider-managed backups until those backups expire according to the applicable provider’s retention process.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Delete your account in the app

Signed-in users can delete their account at any time directly in Games Castle:

  1. Open the app and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account (حذف الحساب).
  3. Confirm, then confirm again with your Google account.

This immediately deletes your sign-in account, your profile and saved progress, virtual currency balances, reward history, and your block list.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal information by emailing:

Email: developera44@gmail.com

Subject: Games Castle — Account Deletion Request

Include the email address associated with your account and, if available, your player ID.

We may ask you to verify ownership before processing a request. Never send your password, verification codes, or full payment card details.

Email requests are reviewed and processed manually, normally within 30 days. Sending an email does not immediately delete the account.

Deletion includes account information and associated personal data that are no longer required. Certain transaction or security records may be retained where necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention, refund processing, or dispute resolution. Any retained information will be limited to those purposes.

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play.
